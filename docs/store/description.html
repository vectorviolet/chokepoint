Hold the line. Build the chokepoint.

Chokepoint is a 2D tower defense game. Defend a winding path through 25 rounds of escalating enemies, from foot soldiers to siege tanks, then keep going in endless mode. Buy, place and upgrade towers, call in bombing runs, and spend every coin where it counts.

Features

  • Four towers with upgrade paths: a rapid gun, a homing missile, a heavy cannon and a bombing plane.
  • Enemies that fight back: armed tanks shoot your towers and can destroy them.
  • 25 rounds, then endless mode. Your run is saved, so you can quit and continue later.
  • Your pace: x1, x2 and x4 speed, auto-pause between rounds, and continuous placement.
  • Click anything to inspect it: a tower's kills, earnings and upgrade preview, or an enemy's HP.
  • Punchy feedback: hit flashes, explosions, rubble and flying coins, with a "reduce flashing" option.
  • English and Turkish, with window, audio and gameplay settings.
  • A live battle plays itself behind the main menu.

Towers and enemies

  • Rapid gun: a cheap starter that fires fast, but with short range and light damage.
  • Homing missile: tracks its target; its damage grows fast with upgrades.
  • Heavy cannon: long range and heavy hits, but it can't hit enemies up close.
  • Plane: flies one pass over the map, dropping splash bombs. One global upgrade makes every plane stronger, even those already in the air.

Six enemy types march in, from the basic grunt to the siege tank. Every number (prices, ranges, damage, enemy HP and each wave's contents) is on the stats wiki.

Controls

  • Buy a tower: click it in the bottom bar, or press 1-4. Left click a green tile to place it; right click cancels.
  • Inspect, upgrade or sell: click a tower, then the arrow or the money bag.
  • Keep placing: Shift.
  • Start or pause the rounds: Space.
  • Upgrade the planes: U.
  • Scroll and zoom: move the mouse to a screen edge; mouse wheel.
  • Hide the HUD: Tab. Fullscreen: F11. Pause menu: Esc.

Installing

Download the zip for your system, unzip it and run the game. No Python or installer needed.

  • Windows: run chokepoint.exe. The game isn't code-signed, so the first launch may show "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
  • macOS: open Chokepoint.app. The app isn't signed, so macOS blocks the first launch: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway next to Chokepoint, then Open.
  • Linux: run ./chokepoint/chokepoint.
